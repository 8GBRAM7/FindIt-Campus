How it works

Three steps to a reunion

Most items on the board find their way home within a day.

  1. 01

    Post or search

    Lost it? File a report with a description. Found it? Snap a photo and post it in seconds.

  2. 02

    Match and message

    Owners claim an item from the board. Contact details unlock only after a claim, so nothing is public.

  3. 03

    Meet and collect

    Pick a busy, well-lit spot like the Student Union, check the details and hand it back.
